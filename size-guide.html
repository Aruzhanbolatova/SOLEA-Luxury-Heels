How to measure your foot

  1. Place your foot on a piece of paper.
  2. Mark the longest point of your foot from heel to toe.
  3. Measure the distance between the heel and the longest toe.
  4. Compare your measurement with the SOLEA size chart.
TIP: If your measurement falls between chart entries, start with the next listed size. Check the specific designer’s guide, as fit varies by style.
